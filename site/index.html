Features

A week with Clawde

Six steps that repeat every week. Each one feeds the next plan, so the longer you train, the better it fits.

Start with a chat

Clawde asks what you're training for, how much time you have and what kit you've got. Ten questions at most, then a summary you can correct.

Your week in one tap

Pick your days and session length. Coach Clawde writes a prompt for your AI chat with your goal, injuries and recent training. Paste the reply back and the week is set.

Log every set as you go

Log reps and weight against each target. The rest timer starts itself and beeps when it's time to go, and every exercise has a how-to guide.

Run Mode times every rep

Press Start and run. A tone marks every change, and paces come from a recent race time. When you finish, the log has filled itself in.

A minute at the end of the week

How did the week go? Is the knee better or worse? Your answers shape the next plan, and you can always skip.

Watch it add up

See where you are in the block, your last four weeks and your best set for every lift.

And the small things

Sign in on your phone, tablet or laptop and your plans and history are there.

Bring your own plan

Paste a plan from your coach or a book and Clawde converts it. Or just log as you go.

FAQ

Questions

Who writes the plans?

The AI chat you already pay for: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or any other. There's no extra subscription. Coach Clawde writes one prompt from your goal, injuries, recent training and a set of coaching rules, and copies it for you. One tap opens it in Claude, or you can paste it into any other chat. Paste the reply back and the app turns it into your sessions.

Where does my data go?

Into your own private account, and only you can see it. Your plans, workouts and answers aren't shared with anyone. They only leave the app when you send a prompt to your AI chat yourself.

How do I get an invite?

Invites go out by hand while the app is tested. If you'd like to join, email n473.cl0@gmail.com. The code is public too: the repo includes a setup guide if you'd like to run your own copy.

Is this made by Anthropic?

No. Coach Clawde is an independent project. Clawde the crab is a nod to the Claude Code mascot, now wearing a sweatband.

Is it medical advice?

No. It programs around the injuries you describe and keeps things conservative until you're cleared, but it isn't medical advice. If an injury is serious, recent or getting worse, see a GP or physio before you train, and stop if anything causes sharp pain.
